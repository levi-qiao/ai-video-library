Locked-off super wide at pad deck level, the camera almost on the plating, a cleat and a painted arc sharp in the near foreground. The frame never moves. Cold desaturated blue grey palette. The dropship is a heavy four-strut lander with a banded stern drum and a lit orange doorway. BEAT 1: The dropship descends beyond the far rim of the pad, lamp cones swinging across the deck. Mist lies undisturbed. A single dead leaf sits stuck flat in the water film. BEAT 2: The wash arrives. Mist blows apart from the centre outward in one spreading ring, the leaf lifts and skates the length of the deck and over the rim, and standing water tears off the plating in flat sheets traveling toward camera, the painted circle surfacing behind them. BEAT 3: Heat refraction behind the stern drum bends the settlement modules and the jungle wall into shimmer. The edge lights smear to streaks through the spray as the dropship rotates a few degrees to square with the circle. BEAT 4: The near strut bites first. The hull rocks, then settles level, all four pads compressing under the weight, water flooding out from beneath each one toward the rim. BEAT 5: The wash cuts out. Water and steam roll back inward, the edge lights sharpen, mist reclaims the clearing from the edges. The drum banding glows dull orange and fades. A last frond flutters down onto the plating.
